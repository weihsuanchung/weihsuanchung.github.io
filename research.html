Research

Non-Hermitian Topological Phenomena — MPL

Research Intern · Sep.–Oct. 2026 · Lise Meitner Research Group, Dr. Flore Kunst

Selected for an eight-week internship at the Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light in Erlangen, Germany. The project focuses on theoretical research into non-Hermitian and topological phenomena in open quantum systems using analytical and numerical methods.

Robustness of Error-Correction Codes to Realistic Noise — MPQ

Summer Research Intern · Aug. 2026 · Supervisor: Dr. Rahul Trivedi

Selected as one of 13 participants from 386 applicants for the MCQST Summer Bachelor Program at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics. Investigated quantum error-correcting codes under realistic noise models, using Python and Stim to simulate quantum errors, syndrome measurements, and error-correction procedures.

Waveguide QED & Open Quantum Systems — IAMS, Academia Sinica

Research Assistant · Jul. 2025–present · Advisor: Dr. Hsiang-Hua Jen

Performed numerical simulations of excitation dynamics and spectral properties in structured waveguide QED using Python and Julia; contributed to simulation, analysis, and manuscript writing for a Physical Review A publication. Current work investigates Dicke-state generation through quantum-coherence control in directional waveguide QED, alongside non-equilibrium phenomena including the Quantum Mpemba Effect and quantum batteries in waveguide and cavity QED systems.

Physics Education & Luminer — National Taiwan University

Research Assistant · Mar.–Jul. 2025; Feb.–Jun. 2026 · Advisor: Prof. Pei-Yun Yang

Developed Luminer, an AI teaching assistant for introductory physics, using Python, Streamlit, and the Gemini API. Analyzed student–chatbot interactions to evaluate system performance and educational effectiveness. Reviewed Just-in-Time Teaching literature and analyzed student feedback and performance data; co-first author of a manuscript in preparation.

Reciprocal Forces in Granular Systems — IOP, Academia Sinica

Research Assistant · Aug. 2024–Jul. 2025 · Advisor: Dr. Kiwing To

Conducted experiments on reciprocal-force behavior in granular systems and developed Linux shell scripts for experimental data processing and analysis. Continued the project independently by developing LAMMPS simulations.
